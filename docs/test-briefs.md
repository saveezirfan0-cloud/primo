# Test briefs

Paste any of these into **New Estimate**. Each mixes features from more than one library job so the
matcher has to blend, not copy. Expected behaviour is noted so you know what a good result looks like.

## 1. Mixed hall: new audio system + dual projection (blends 6570 audio with 6401 video)

> School hall AV upgrade, Greenfield PS. Two new 8,000 lm laser projectors on ceiling poles with two new
> motorised screens; remove the existing side projectors and screens. Three HDMI inputs: stage, rack and
> rear of hall, auto-switching with HDBaseT extenders. New 4-speaker audio system with new 4-channel amp,
> new DSP with Dante, digital stage box with 3 wired mic inputs. 4 wireless mics (2 handheld, 2 lapel)
> with remote antennas. Wireless presentation from teacher laptops. Two touch panels, one at the rack and
> one at the stage. New 37RU rack. Integrate the existing hearing loop. EWP needed.

Expect: 6570 and 6401 both match well, single-section pricing in the $75k to $95k range, new speakers and
amp from the price book, two projectors and screens, 4 wireless channels.

## 2. Staged hall in three stages (6401 structure, 6570 equipment)

> Hall upgrade, Westbrook HS, priced in three stages so the school can spread it over two budget years.
> Stage 1: video only, one 8,000 lm laser projector, keep the existing screen, two HDMI inputs at stage and
> rack, temporary manual operation with the remote. Stage 2: control, one 7" touch panel, control
> processor, network switch, relay for the screen, integrate the lighting system. Stage 3: audio, new DSP,
> reuse the 4 existing speakers and amp, 2 wireless handhelds, 1 wired lectern mic, Bluetooth input.
> Reuse the existing rack. EWP needed. Remove the old projector and DVD player.

Expect: three sections named by stage, Stage 1 close to 6427 video, Stage 3 close to 6427 audio, lighting
integration pulling a lighting interface from 6401 or 6521.

## 3. Auditorium refresh with options (6521 pattern)

> Auditorium AV upgrade, Riverside HS. Keep the existing projector, screen, speakers and subs. Replace the
> digital mixing console and stage box with new Dante DSP and stage I/O, new 4-channel amp, 4 wireless mics,
> 1 wired mic, wireless presentation, video distribution to 3 inputs. New 27RU rack. Integrate the existing
> lighting controller and hearing augmentation. Two touch panels, bio box and stage. EWP needed.
> Optional items: stage camera with recording and streaming, console training session, lighting audit.

Expect: 6521 ranked first with a high score, single section around $65k to $80k, optional items listed
separately and not in the subtotal.

## 4. Small job, video only (tests scaling down)

> Small hall, Parkview PS. Replace the projector only with a 6,500 lm laser projector on the existing pole.
> Keep the screen. One HDMI input at the rack with a wall plate at the stage. No audio or control changes.
> EWP needed.

Expect: one section, roughly $8k to $13k, mostly price book projector plus labour hours from standards,
minimal overheads because equipment value is low.

## 5. Out of library (tests honesty)

> Classroom AV, Hilltop PS, 6 classrooms. Each room gets a 75" interactive display on a wall mount, a
> soundbar, one HDMI wall plate and a small control keypad. No projectors, no rack.

Expect: low match scores and a warning, many lines marked ai guess or missing, because there are no
classroom jobs in the library yet. This is the scenario to show Nof to explain why more proposals matter.

## Reading the result

- Score above 0.7 means a close template; below 0.5 means treat the draft as a rough shape only.
- The basis badge on each line says where the number came from. Green price book and rate card lines are
  reliable. Ratio estimate lines are proportional guesses. Ai guess lines need a manual price.
- Compare the section subtotals to the nearest past job in the Library to sanity check the overall size.
